Discharge Curve Digitizer

Turns a voltage discharge chart image into a Voltage vs. SoC table you can paste into Excel. Everything runs in this page and nothing is uploaded.

1. Load chart image

Or paste a screenshot (Win+Shift+S, then Ctrl+V here), or drag an image onto the page. For PDFs, zoom the PDF in before you take the screenshot. Higher resolution gives better accuracy.

3. Trace curves

Color tolerance 60
Max gap to bridge (px)
Auto-trace follows the clicked color left and right. It bridges dashes and places where other curves cross over. If it jumps onto the wrong curve or the legend, lower the tolerance, use Limit trace area, or use Erase box to remove the bad points. Then fix them by hand with Add/remove points (left-click adds, right-click removes).
